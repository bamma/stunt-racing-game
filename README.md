# Zoom Zoom Stunt Race 🏎️🌈

A silly, kid-friendly 3D racing game in the browser, made with [three.js](https://threejs.org/).
You see your car from behind. Race three cartoon cars with googly eyes to the giant trophy and do crazy stunts on the way!

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. It needs an internet connection because three.js loads from a CDN.
It runs as-is on GitHub Pages. There's no build step: set Pages to deploy from the `main` branch, root folder.

## Controls

| Keyboard | Touch | Action |
|---|---|---|
| ◀ ▶ or A D | ◀ ▶ buttons | Steer |
| Space, ▲ or W | 🤸 button | Hop. In the air: **flip!** (hold to keep flipping) |
| ▼ or S | 🐢 button | Slow down |
| H | 📯 button | Honk (the other cars honk back!) |
| M | | Music on / off |
| Esc or P | ⏸ | Pause |

The car speeds up by itself, so small kids only need to steer.

## The course

1. **Big Jump** over a pond with giant rubber ducks. Press jump in the air to do flips and get a turbo boost.
2. **Loop-de-loop**, upside down!
3. **Rainbow Pipe**: drive all the way around the inside of a glass tube. Steer around the sleepy pillows that block part of the pipe. The stars show the safe way. If you bonk the same pillow twice, it yawns and rolls away, so nobody gets stuck.
4. **Barrel Roll**: the road twists all the way around.
5. **Mega Jump** through a rainbow.
6. **Bumpy Road** with beach balls to bonk.
7. Finish line and a big golden trophy!

Along the way there are ⭐ stars to collect, plus blue boost pads.

## Kid-friendly by design

- You can't crash or fall off. Walls are bouncy pillows, and if a jump comes up short, a friendly bird puts you back on the road.
- Ramps give exactly the right speed, so every jump lands.
- The other racers adjust their speed to stay close, so the race stays exciting but is easy to win.
- Spoken call-outs ("Loop de loop!") help kids who can't read yet. They use the browser's built-in speech, if available.
- Every finish place is celebrated.
